# MediScanAi

**Prescription scanner & medicine reminder app for families in India, English + Hindi, coming soon to Android.**

## What it does

- **Prescription scan** – photograph a prescription and get it in a clear, readable form.
- **Reminders & dose tracking** – medicine reminders, with a record of doses taken.
- **Family profiles** – keep health information for the whole family in one place.
- **Medicine interaction check** – see possible interactions between the medicines you add.
- **Doctor Summary** – a short summary you can share with your doctor.
- **Hindi + English** – use the app in either language.

## FAQ

**What is MediScanAi?**
A family health app for Android, built for India. It helps you understand prescriptions, remember medicines and keep your family's medicine details organised.

**Is MediScanAi a doctor?**
No. It is an information assistant and does not diagnose, treat or cure. Always follow your doctor's advice.
