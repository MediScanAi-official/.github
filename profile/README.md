# MediScanAi

**Prescription scanner and medicine reminder app for families in India. English + Hindi. Coming soon to Android.**

MediScanAi is a family health assistant. It helps you understand prescriptions, remember medicines on time and keep your whole family's medicine details in one place.

## What it does

- **Prescription scan:** photograph a prescription and see the medicines in a clear, readable list.
- **Medicine reminders:** set reminders and track every dose.
- **Family profiles:** keep medicines for parents, children and grandparents separate and organised.
- **Medicine interaction check:** get information about possible interactions between medicines.
- **Doctor visit summary:** create a simple PDF summary to share with your doctor.
- **Hindi + English:** use the app in the language you are comfortable with.
